Clinic Location

Shown on the booking page for in-clinic visits, and emailed to patients after they book an in-clinic visit (video visits don't get it).

In Google Maps, find your clinic → Share → Copy link, and paste it here. This is the link patients tap for directions. If empty, we search Google Maps for the address.
For an exact pin: Google Maps → Share → Embed a map → Copy HTML, and paste it here. If empty, the map shows your address.
