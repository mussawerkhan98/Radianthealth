    Questions, answered

    Can't find what you're looking for? Our front desk is happy to help on WhatsApp.

    How do I book an appointment?

    Use our online booking: choose a department and doctor, pick an available time, and enter your details. You can also call or message us on WhatsApp.

    Do I need an account to book?

    No — you can book as a guest. A free account lets you see upcoming and past visits and your medical history in the patient portal.

    How do I cancel or reschedule?

    Log in to your patient portal to cancel an upcoming appointment, or message our front desk and we'll help you reschedule.
