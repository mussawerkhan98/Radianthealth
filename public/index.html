FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Contact Us
How do I book an appointment?

Use our online booking page: choose a department and doctor, pick an available time, and enter your details. You can also call or message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need an account to book?

No — you can book as a guest. Creating a free account lets you see upcoming and past visits and your medical history in the patient portal.

How do I cancel or change my appointment?

Log in to your patient portal to cancel an upcoming appointment, or contact our front desk and we'll help you reschedule.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

Coverage depends on your plan. Please contact us with your insurance details before your visit and we'll confirm for you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if any), a list of current medications, and any recent test results or reports.
